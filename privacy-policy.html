Privacy Policy — ARIA

Last updated: September 2026

ARIA ("the App") is a personal desktop assistant developed by Hassaan Ali. This page explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Information We Collect

If you create an account to use ARIA's cloud features, we collect:

  • Email address — used only to identify your account and let you log in from any computer.
  • Password — never stored or seen by us in readable form. It is handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores only a securely encrypted version of it.
  • Reminders you create — the text and time of reminders you ask ARIA to set, so they can sync across devices.
  • Chat history — the messages you exchange with ARIA, so your conversation can be restored when you reopen the App.

If you do not create an account, ARIA also works fully offline/locally, and in that mode none of the above is sent anywhere.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your microphone recordings — voice commands are transcribed locally by your operating system's speech tools or a speech-to-text service, and are not stored by ARIA.
  • We do not track your location, browsing history, or other apps you use.
  • We do not collect payment or financial information.

3. How Your Information Is Used

  • To let you log in and access your reminders/chat history from any device.
  • To make ARIA's assistant features (reminders, conversation memory) work as intended.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with advertisers or third parties for marketing.

4. Third-Party Services Used

ARIA relies on the following third-party services to function. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data passed to it:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Realtime Database) — stores your account and synced data. Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API and/or Anthropic Claude API — used to generate conversational AI replies when you chat with ARIA. The text of your message is sent to whichever provider is configured, per their respective policies.
  • Open-Meteo, Google News, Frankfurter, Wikipedia, MyMemory, CoinGecko — free public data APIs used to answer weather, news, currency, encyclopedia, translation, and cryptocurrency-price questions. These requests typically contain only the query text (e.g., a city name), not your identity.

5. Data Security

Passwords are hashed/encrypted by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us in plain text. Reminders and chat history are stored under an access rule that only allows your own logged-in account to read or write them.

6. Your Choices

  • You can use ARIA without creating an account; local features work without sending any data to the cloud.
  • You can log out at any time using the Logout button in the App.
  • To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

ARIA is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of ARIA after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.
